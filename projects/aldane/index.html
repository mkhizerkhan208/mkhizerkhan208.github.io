Open heart · 5 o'clock

A window onto the balance

The balance wheel swings back and forth four times a second, which is 28,800 vibrations an hour. The seconds hand counts that rhythm in eight small steps per second.

The blued hairspring under the wheel keeps the time. Change its length by a hair and the watch gains or loses seconds each day.

From the sapphire crystal down to the exhibition caseback. The movement keeps running while it's apart, and the gears speed up so you can follow the power from barrel to balance.

Wound by your wrist

An 18k rose-gold rotor swings with every movement of your arm and winds the mainspring in both directions. Fully wound, the watch runs for 72 hours.
