Good to know

Questions, answered.

How far ahead should I book an event?+

Two to three weeks is ideal for private hire. Classes and tastings can usually be booked up to 48 hours before.

Can you handle dietary requirements?+

Yes. We offer oat, almond and lactose-free milk, and our chocolatier makes vegan and gluten-free truffles on request.

Do you travel outside Brooklyn?+

Our coffee cart covers all five boroughs and northern New Jersey. Travel beyond 25 miles is quoted separately.

Is there a deposit?+

A 25% deposit secures your date, fully refundable up to 14 days before the event.
