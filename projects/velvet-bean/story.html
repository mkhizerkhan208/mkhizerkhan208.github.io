Timeline

Six years,
one obsession.

2019

A cart on Bedford Ave

Maya and Theo start selling espresso and homemade truffles from a converted flower cart.

2021

Our first roaster

A 15kg drum roaster moves into a Greenpoint garage. We start sourcing directly from farms.

2023

The bar on Wythe

We open the flagship: velvet chairs, a glass chocolate kitchen and a bar that stays open late.

2026

The atelier

Bean-to-bar chocolate goes fully in-house, from cacao pod to the truffle on your saucer.

What we stand for

Three promises.

Paid fairly

We pay farmers an average of 2.4x the commodity price and publish every contract.

Low waste

Compostable cups, grounds to local gardens and cacao husks brewed into tea.

Good jobs

Living wages, health cover and paid barista training for every member of the team.
